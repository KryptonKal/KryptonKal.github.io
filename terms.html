Personal use only

Jarvis is a personal assistant built for and used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered, sold or provided to anyone else, and no one else may sign in to it.

What it does

With the owner's permission, Jarvis reads the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar (read-only) to read out mail and events on request. It cannot send, change or delete anything. How that data is handled is described in the privacy policy.

No warranty

Jarvis is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. Its owner uses it at their own discretion.

Ending use

Access to Google data can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
