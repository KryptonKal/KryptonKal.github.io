Jarvis Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Jarvis is a personal assistant used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. This policy describes how it handles Google user data.

What Jarvis accesses

Jarvis requests two read-only permissions: Gmail (read-only) and Google Calendar (read-only). It uses them only to read out the owner's recent mail and calendar events when the owner asks. It cannot send, modify or delete email or calendar events.

How the data is used and stored

  • Mail and calendar data is read on request and shown or spoken to the owner on the owner's own computer.
  • It is not stored, apart from the sign-in token Google issues, which is kept on that computer only.
  • It is not sold, shared, or transferred to anyone, and not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • Mail and calendar content is not sent to any AI service.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email address shown on Jarvis's Google sign-in screen.
